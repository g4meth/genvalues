What is GenValues?

GenValues is a political quiz that attempts to assign percentages for twelve different political values pertaining to the GenUSA discord server. You will be presented by a statement, and then you will answer with your opinion on the statement, from Strongly Agree to Strongly Disagree, with each answer slightly affecting your scores. At the end of the quiz, your answers will be compared to the maximum possible for each value, thus giving you a percentage. Answer honestly! This test is a fork of 8values.

There are 70 questions in the test.

What are the twelve values?

There are six independent axes - Growth, Server Politics, Mod Purge, Culture, Government Structure, and Political Culture - and each has two opposing values assigned to them. They are:

OPEN

Those with higher Open growth scores believe that GenUSA should focus on growing its member count as much as possible, even if these new members don't fit in with the rest of the server politically or culturally.

GROWTH

CLOSED

Those with higher Closed growth scores believe that GenUSA should avoid growing the member count relentlessly, to preserve culture and balance. They often support some growth primarily restricted to the friends of current members.

ACTIVE

Those with higher Active scores care about the server government larp. They typically support reviving the larp and actively care about elections. No matter what party they support, they are inherently better than the subhuman apathetics.

SERVER POLITICS

APATHETIC

Those with higher Apathetic scores do not care abouth the server government and larp. They usually are just here to talk and chill, and don't vote unless they're asked to. They are CATTLE and do not deserve to live.

DEEPSTATE

Those with higher Deepstate scores value strengthening the mod team and admins, and support mod influence over the server government. They are opposed to the concept of a mod purge.

MOD PURGE

KILL THEM ALL

Those with higher Kill Them All scores believe in firing a large chunk of, or all, of the current admins and mods. They support a mod purge, and feel that the current system is inefficient or restrictive.

WOKE

Those with higher Woke scores are culturally to the left of GenUSA. They often support Democratic or Progressive policies in real life, and were less likely to be a member of the Chud Party.

PERSONAL CULTURE

CHUD

Those with higher Chud scores are culturally to the right of GenUSA. They often support Republican or conservative policies in real life, and were more likely to have been a member of the Chud Party.

CENTRALIZED

Those with higher Centralized scores believe that the server government should have a strong executive branch, with the President having significant authority over government decisions. They typically support concentrating power in the presidency to improve efficiency and reduce legislative gridlock.

GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE

DECENTRALIZED

Those with higher Decentralized scores believe that government authority should be divided between multiple independent institutions. They support strong checks and balances, legislative oversight, and limiting the power of the President, even if this makes government less efficient.

SERIOUS

Those with higher Serious scores believe that the ideal political platform is a serious one, modeled like a real political party. They support serious and larpy candidates.

POLITICAL CULTURE

UNSERIOUS

Those with higher Unserious scores believe that server politics should primarily be entertaining rather than realistic. They support joke candidates over serious candidates.

In addition to matching you to the 12 values, the quiz also attempts to match you to some random ideology. Take this part seriously, its the most important part for determining your self worth.
